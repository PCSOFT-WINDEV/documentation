


## &lt;PolygonGeo variable&gt;.Perimeter (Function)

***In french: &lt;Variable PolygoneGéo&gt;.Périmètre***



<a name="XUse"></a>
<a name="Use"></a>
<a name="description"></a>
Calculates the perimeter of a geographic polygon. 

**Remarks**: 

- The perimeter of a polygon is the sum of the length of its exterior and interior rings.

- The perimeter of a multipolygon is the sum of the perimeters of its polygons.



<a name="XSYNTAX"></a>

## Syntax
<a name="SYNTAX1"></a>

`<Result> = <Geometry>.Perimeter()`
---

**`<Result>: (Real)`**



- Perimeter of the specified geometry. This geometry must be a polygon or multipolygon. 

	- For geometries defined with 2D coordinates, the perimeter is calculated based on the Euclidean (Pythagorean) distance. The unit is the same as the one used for the point coordinates.

	- For geometries defined using geographic coordinates, the perimeter is calculated using a reference system that takes into account the curvature of the earth. The perimeter is returned in meters.




- 0 in other cases (geometry other than a polygon or multipolygon).




**`<Geometry>: (PolygonGeo variable)`**

Name of the [PolygonGeo](../WDLang4/1410090127.md) variable to be used.
