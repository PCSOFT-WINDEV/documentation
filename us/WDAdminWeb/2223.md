## ERR_NO_TABLE error
<a name="errnotable_error_ELTTEXTE000061"></a>
The &lt;COMMAND_NAME&gt; command only operates with the tables and &lt;CONTROL_NAME&gt; is not a table. ERR_NO_TABLE (37)

The WD_ACTION_ parameter was used on a control other than a TABLE control.
Check the custom actions found in your page.
