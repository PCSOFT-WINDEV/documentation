## ERR_BAD_WEBDEV_CONFIG error

### ERR_BAD_WEBDEV_CONFIG: general misconfiguration of WEBDEV. The &lt;KEYNAME&gt; key is missing or incorrect. 
<a name="errbadwebdevconfig_general_misconfiguration_webdev_the_key_missing_incorrect_ELTPARAGRAPHE000007"></a>

The WEBDEV setup was not performed properly in the registry.

**Remark**: WEBDEV configuration information is stored in the registry under "HKEY_LOCAL_MACHINE\\SOFTWARE\\PC SOFT\\WEBDEV\\31".. 
An entry is created for each new dynamic WEBDEV site.
