## Seminars within your company
<a name="seminars_within_your_company_ELTTEXTE000209"></a>
PC SOFT can organize training seminars within your company, upon request.

The content of seminar can even be customized.

The duration can vary between 2 and 5 days and up to 8 participants can attend these training sessions.

For more details, visit our website: [www.windev.com](https://www.windev.com/training/in-house-training-sessions.htm)

## Consulting (Assistance)
<a name="consulting_assistance_ELTTEXTE000233"></a>
PC SOFT offers a unique assistance package: for a period of 1 to 5 days, we put at your disposal a developer who is familiar with your field and the tools you use.

This enables you to start a new application with the help from an experienced developer.

This consulting session takes place via video conference.

For more details, visit the website: [www.windev.com](https://www.windev.com/ts/assistance.htm)
