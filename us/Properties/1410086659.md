
## BrightnessAuto

***In french: LuminositéModifie***
	



<a name="XUtilisation"></a>
<a name="Utilisation"></a>
<a name="description"></a>
The **BrightnessAuto** keyword allows you to adjust brightness on an Android or iOS device. This keyword can correspond to: 

- the [BrightnessAuto](../WDLang3/1000020182.md) function. 

- the *brightnessAuto* constant, which can be used with [BrightnessModify](../WDLang3/1000020170.md).
