
## GglAccountDissociate (Function)

***In french: GglCompteDissocie***



<a name="XUtilisation"></a>
<a name="Utilisation"></a>
<a name="description"></a>
Dissociates the application from the user's Google account. This association must have been previously defined with [GglAccountAssociate](../WDLang3/1410089808.md).

Reminder: This association enables one-click authentication in the application with a Google account registered on the device, without the need to enter a login and password.

<a name="sample_code"></a>

GglAccountDissociate()
