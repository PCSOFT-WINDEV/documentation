
## Constants for managing Beacons
			

<a name="NOTE1"></a>
<a name="NOTE1_1"></a>
The following constants are used to manage Beacons:

| Constant | Details | Function/Type |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **bdbEnter** | The device entered the transmission range of a group of Beacons. | [BeaconDetectBackground](../WDLang3/1000023112.md) |
| **bdbLeave** | The device left the transmission range of a group of Beacons. | [BeaconDetectBackground](../WDLang3/1000023112.md) |
| **biDistant** | Proximity of the beacon to the device: beacon beyond 3 meters. | [beaconDetectionInfo](../WDLang3/1000023109.md) type |
| **biNear** | Proximity of the beacon to the device: beacon between 0.5 and 3 meters. | [beaconDetectionInfo](../WDLang3/1000023109.md) type |
| **biVeryClose** | Proximity of Beacon to device: tag less than 0.5 m away. | [beaconDetectionInfo](../WDLang3/1000023109.md) type |
