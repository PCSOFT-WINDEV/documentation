<a name="NOTE2_1"></a>


## Banner settings
<a name="banner_settings_ELTTEXTE000097"></a>
In these settings, you can specify: 

- the title displayed in the banner. 

- the image displayed next to the title. 

- the banner and title colors. 




These settings will be applied next time the help pages are generated. 

Bote: By default, the title corresponds to the name of the help file and is displayed as black text over a gray banner.
