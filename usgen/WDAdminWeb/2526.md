## ERR_BAD_DLLEXE error
<a name="errbaddllexe_error_ELTTEXTE000063"></a>
The &lt;DLL_NAME&gt; DLL is not compatible with this WEBDEV version. ERR_BAD_DLLEXE 

This message is displayed if:

- A conflict occurs between the DLLs installed on the computer.

- The 32-bit DLLs of WEBDEV are not installed properly. Reinstall WEBDEV.
