


## Reorder the data displayed in your tables
			



<a name="NOTE1"></a>
<a name="NOTE1_1"></a>
You want to rearrange the columns in your table? It's easier than ever in a WINDEV application. Simply perform the following operations:

1. Click the heading of the column you want to move, and hold the left mouse button down.

2. Move the column to its new position and release the left mouse button. An animation allows you to view the movement of the column.

### Tip: Display only certain columns
<a name="tip_display_only_certain_columns_ELTPARAGRAPHE000016"></a>

In some cases, a simple move is not enough: some information is unnecessary. You can choose the columns displayed in the tables of your application. You just have to hide the columns you don't want to be displayed. 

To choose the columns that will be displayed in your tables: 

1. Open the context menu of the table. 

2. Select the option "Select columns". 

3. Specify the columns to display (a checkmark indicates which columns are shown). 

<br>![](https://doc.pcsoft.fr/en-US/images/image.awp?langid=214&name=Indiquer%20les%20colonnes%20%E0%20afficher1.jpg)


For example: The table below displays the columns "Company", "Last name", "First name", "Postal code", "City" and "Country". To view the "City" and "Country" columns, you need to use the horizontal scrollbar.
