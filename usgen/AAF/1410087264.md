


## Customize your charts: Type, color, legends, etc.
			



<a name="NOTE1"></a>
<a name="NOTE1_1"></a>
You want to change the type of chart used in your application? The data is displayed in a line chart but you need a column chart?

Use the context menu of the chart. <br>![](https://doc.pcsoft.fr/en-US/images/image.awp?langid=214&name=Personnalisez%20vos%20graphes.jpg)


This menu shows the different types of graphs available: simply click on the desired type to modify the graph currently displayed in your application.
