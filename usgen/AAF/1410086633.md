
## Sort the data displayed in your tables in one click
			

<a name="NOTE1"></a>
<a name="NOTE1_1"></a>
**You want to sort the table content by column?** Simply click the column heading.

A double arrow icon appears on sortable columns.

When the sort is enabled, a small arrow icon indicates the column sort direction.<br>![](https://doc.pcsoft.fr/en-US/images/image.awp?langid=214&name=Triez%20les%20donn%E9es%20affich%E9es%20dans%20vos%20tables%20en%20un%20clic.jpg)


To sort a table on several columns: 

1. Hold the Ctrl key down. 

2. Click the headings of the columns to be sorted. Caution: the columns must be selected according to the requested order. For example, to sort by company and by name, you must click the "Company" column and then the "Name" column.
