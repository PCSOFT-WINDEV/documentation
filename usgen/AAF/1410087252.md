


## Print the content of your tables
			



<a name="NOTE1"></a>
<a name="NOTE1_1"></a>
To directly print the data displayed in a table, simply select "Print" in the context menu. You can immediately view the data from your table in the report viewer. Then, you can directly print the data on your printer. <br>![Print options](https://doc.pcsoft.fr/en-US/images/image.awp?langid=214&name=Imprimez%20directement%20le%20contenu%20de%20vos%20tables.jpg)
