


## HTTPCookieManage (Function)

***In french: HTTPCookieGère***



<a name="XUtilisation"></a>
<a name="Utilisation"></a>
<a name="description"></a>
Enables or disables the management of cookies in an HTTP request run: 

- by the [HTTPRequest](../WDLang3/3043007.md) function. 

- ![WINDEV](https://doc.pcsoft.fr/ext/images/us/WD.png)![WEBDEV - Server code](https://doc.pcsoft.fr/ext/images/us/WBS.png)![Android](https://doc.pcsoft.fr/ext/images/us/ANDROID.png)![Android Widget ](https://doc.pcsoft.fr/ext/images/us/ANDROIDWIDGET.png)![iPhone/iPad](https://doc.pcsoft.fr/ext/images/us/IPHONE.png)![IOS Widget](https://doc.pcsoft.fr/ext/images/us/WIDGETIOS.png)![Mac Catalyst](https://doc.pcsoft.fr/ext/images/us/CATALYST.png)![Ajax](https://doc.pcsoft.fr/ext/images/us/AJAX.png) by the [HTTPSend](../WDLang3/1000021183.md) or [RESTSend](../WDLang3/1000021476.md) function. 


**Note** Cookies read and sent by the [HTTPRequest](../WDLang3/3043007.md) function are only stored in the application's memory. They are not related to the cookies of Web browsers installed on the computer.




<a name="Exemple1"></a>
<a name="sample_code"></a>

## Example


```wl
// Manages the cookies in the requests on the sites of the "google.fr" domain
HTTPCookieManage(".google.fr", True)

// Open the home page of Google
HTTPRequest("www.google.com")

// Re-runs the request while taking into account the cookies possibly returned during the 1st call
HTTPRequest("www.google.fr/search?hl=fr&source=hp&q=windev")
```

## Syntax
<a name="SYNTAXE1"></a>

### Enabling/Disabling the management of cookies (HTTPRequest)

`HTTPCookieManage([<Domain>, ] <Activation>)`
---

**`<Domain>: (Character string)`**

Domain onto which the activation or the deactivation is applied.

**`<Activation>: (Boolean)`**



- <u><u><u><u>True</u></u></u></u> to enable the management of cookies,

- <u><u><u><u>False</u></u></u></u> to disable the management of cookies.
	Even if the management of cookies is disabled, you still have the ability to pass cookies in the HTTP request by using the appropriate header in [HTTPRequest](../WDLang3/3043007.md). Similarly, the cookies returned by the server can be retrieved by [HTTPGetResult](../WDLang3/3043001.md).

### Enabling/Disabling the management of cookies (HTTPSend)

`HTTPCookieManage(<HTTP request> [, <Domain>] , <Activation>)`
---

**`<HTTP request>: (httpRequest variable)`**

Name of the [httpRequest](../WDLang3/1000021158.md) variable for which the management of cookies will be enabled or disabled.  

**`<Domain>: (Character string)`**

Domain onto which the activation or the deactivation is applied.

**`<Activation>: (Boolean)`**



- <u><u><u><u>True</u></u></u></u> to enable the management of cookies,

- <u><u><u><u>False</u></u></u></u> to disable the management of cookies.
	Even if the management of cookies is disabled, you still have the ability to pass cookies in the HTTP request by using the appropriate header in the [httpRequest](../WDLang3/1000021158.md) variable.

## Remarks


- The cookies are not kept between two runs of an executable. They must be saved and restored through programming:

	- backup: functions [HTTPCookieGet](../WDLang3/1000019228.md), [Serialize](../WDLang1/3013065.md) and [fSaveText](../WDLang1/3036046.md)

	- restoration: functions [HTTPCookieReplace](../WDLang3/1000019231.md), [Deserialize](../WDLang1/3013066.md) and [fLoadText](../WDLang1/3036006.md).




- [HTTPGetResult](../WDLang3/3043001.md) ignores **HTTPCookieManage**.

- When the cookies are enabled: 

	- the cookies received in response to a call to [HTTPRequest](../WDLang3/3043007.md) are stored while waiting for a future call to [HTTPRequest](../WDLang3/3043007.md) on the same domain. This operating mode is similar to the one of the Internet browsers.

	- ![WINDEV](https://doc.pcsoft.fr/ext/images/us/WD.png)![WEBDEV - Server code](https://doc.pcsoft.fr/ext/images/us/WBS.png)![Android](https://doc.pcsoft.fr/ext/images/us/ANDROID.png)![Android Widget ](https://doc.pcsoft.fr/ext/images/us/ANDROIDWIDGET.png)![iPhone/iPad](https://doc.pcsoft.fr/ext/images/us/IPHONE.png)![IOS Widget](https://doc.pcsoft.fr/ext/images/us/WIDGETIOS.png)![Mac Catalyst](https://doc.pcsoft.fr/ext/images/us/CATALYST.png)![Ajax](https://doc.pcsoft.fr/ext/images/us/AJAX.png) cookies received in response to a call to the [HTTPSend](../WDLang3/1000021183.md) function are stored pending a subsequent call to the [HTTPSend](../WDLang3/1000021183.md) function on the same domain: the [httpRequest](../WDLang3/1000021158.md) type variable is updated.  This operating mode is similar to the one of the Internet browsers.

	- ![WINDEV](https://doc.pcsoft.fr/ext/images/us/WD.png)![WEBDEV - Server code](https://doc.pcsoft.fr/ext/images/us/WBS.png)![Android](https://doc.pcsoft.fr/ext/images/us/ANDROID.png)![Android Widget ](https://doc.pcsoft.fr/ext/images/us/ANDROIDWIDGET.png)![iPhone/iPad](https://doc.pcsoft.fr/ext/images/us/IPHONE.png)![IOS Widget](https://doc.pcsoft.fr/ext/images/us/WIDGETIOS.png)![Mac Catalyst](https://doc.pcsoft.fr/ext/images/us/CATALYST.png)![Ajax](https://doc.pcsoft.fr/ext/images/us/AJAX.png) cookies received in response to a call to the [RESTSend](../WDLang3/1000021476.md) function are stored pending a subsequent call to the [RESTSend](../WDLang3/1000021476.md) function on the same domain: the [restRequest](../WDLang3/1000021481.md) type variable is updated.  This operating mode is similar to the one of the Internet browsers.




- The use of cookies is required on some sites that use them to supply the identifiers. Then, these identifiers are transmitted to each request (a basket identifier when placing an order for example).

- If a management is defined on a domain, this information has priority over the global option.
