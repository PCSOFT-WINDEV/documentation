


## WLanguage procedure called by SensorDetectChangeAcceleration



<a name="XUtilisation"></a>
<a name="Utilisation"></a>
<a name="description"></a>
Procedure ("callback") called by [SensorDetectChangeAcceleration](../WDLang1/1000019362.md) when the acceleration of the device on one of its axes is detected. This procedure can be a local, global or internal procedure.
<a name="Exemple1"></a>
<a name="sample_code"></a>

## Example


```wl
// Detect the changes of acceleration
SensorDetectChangeAcceleration("ProcDetectAcceleration", snrVertical)
```

```wl
// Procedure called by the SensorDetectChangeAcceleration function	
PROCEDURE ProcDetectAcceleration(VerticalAcceleration is real, ...
	HorizontalAcceleration is real, LateralAcceleration is real)
ToastDisplay(StringBuild("Vertical acceleration detected (%1 m/s²)", VerticalAcceleration))

// Stop detecting the changes of acceleration
SensorDetectChangeAcceleration("", snrVertical)
```

<a name="XSYNTAXE"></a>

## Syntax
<a name="SYNTAXE1"></a>

`SensorDetectChangeAcceleration_Callback(<Vertical> , <Longitudinal> , <Lateral>)`
---

**`<Vertical>: (Real)`**

Measurement of device acceleration on the vertical axis (Z) in m/s².

**`<Longitudinal>: (Real)`**

Measurement of device acceleration on the longitudinal axis (Y) in m/s².

**`<Lateral>: (Real)`**

Measurement of device acceleration on the horizontal axis (X) in m/s².
