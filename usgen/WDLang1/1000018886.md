


## OrganizerDisplayPreviousPeriod (Function)

***In french: AgendaAffichePériodePrécédente***



<a name="XUtilisation"></a>
<a name="Utilisation"></a>
<a name="description"></a>
Moves the time area displayed to the previous area (previous weeks for example) in an Organizer control. The Organizer control is displayed from the day corresponding to the previous time area. 

**Remarks**: 

- **OrganizerDisplayPreviousPeriod** and [OrganizerDisplayNextPeriod](../WDLang1/1000018885.md) allow you to simply set up "Next / Previous" Button controls. These controls allow you to scroll through the time areas displayed by the Organizer control. 

- The displayed period can also be modified by [OrganizerChangeMode](../WDLang1/1000019497.md) and [OrganizerPosition](../WDLang1/1000018889.md).







<a name="Exemple1"></a>
<a name="sample_code"></a>

## Example


```wl
// Passage en affichage au mois
AgendaChangeMode(AGD_MonAgenda, agzMois)
// Affichage du mois en cours
AgendaPosition(AGD_MonAgenda, DateSys())
// Passage au mois précédent
AgendaAffichePériodePrécédente(AGD_MonAgenda)
```
