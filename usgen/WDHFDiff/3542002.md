


## WDHFDiff - Use
			



<a name="NOTE1"></a>
<a name="NOTE1_1"></a>


## Overview
<a name="overview_ELTTEXTE000162"></a>
WDHFDiff is used to compare:

- the structure of two HFSQL tables.

- the data in two HFSQL tables.

- the common tables found in two different directories.




<a name="NOTE2"></a>
<a name="NOTE2_1"></a>


## Comparing two HFSQL tables
<a name="comparing_two_hfsql_tables_ELTTEXTE000186"></a>


### Comparing the structure of two HFSQL tables
<a name="comparing_the_structure_two_hfsql_tables_ELTPARAGRAPHE000022"></a>

To compare the structure of two HFSQL tables:

1. On the "Tools" tab, in the "Database" group, click "WDHFDiff".

2. Click the "Compare 2 files" button.

3. Select the two tables to be compared.

4. If necessary, enter the passwords for the tables to be compared.

5. Click the "Compare the structure" button. The differences found are automatically listed:

	- differences in the WLanguage properties of the tables compared.

	- list of items present in the first table, in the second table and common to both tables.

	- differences in the WLanguage properties of the fields in the compared tables.






<a name="NOTE2_2"></a>


### Comparing the data found in two HFSQL tables
<a name="comparing_the_data_found_two_hfsql_tables_ELTPARAGRAPHE000051"></a>

To compare the tables found in two HFSQL tables:

1. On the "Tools" tab, in the "Database" group, click "WDHFDiff".

2. Compare the structure of the two tables. For more details, see the above paragraph.

3. Click the "Compare the data" button.

4. Select the type of comparison to perform:

	- comparison by record number. The records found in the tables will be compared according to their identifier.

	- comparison by unique key. Records in the tables will be compared according to the unique key(s) common to both tables.
			Note: In this type of comparison, crossed-out and/or deleted records will not be compared.




5. Select the items to compare.
	Reminder: Use the "All" button to select all available headings. The "None" button is used to deselect the selected items.

6. Click "OK". The differences found are automatically listed.

7. Double-click a difference to find out its characteristics.

## Comparing the common tables found in two directories
<a name="comparing_the_common_tables_found_two_directories_ELTTEXTE000216"></a>
To compare the common tables found in two different directories:

1. On the "Tools" tab, in the "Database" group, click "WDHFDiff".

2. Click the "Compare 2 directories" button.

3. Select the two directories to compare.

4. Click the "Scan" button. The tables common to the two selected directories are automatically listed.

5. Select the tables to compare.
	Reminder: The "All" button selects all available tables. The "None" button deselects the selected tables.

6. Click the "Compare" button. The number of differences found in each table is automatically displayed.

7. Double-click on a table to see its differences:

	- differences in the WLanguage properties of the tables compared.

	- list of items present in the first table, in the second table and common to both tables.

	- differences in the WLanguage properties of the fields in the compared tables.




8. Click the "Compare the data" button to perform a comparison by record number on the tables. The differences found are automatically listed.

9. Double-click a difference to find out its characteristics.
